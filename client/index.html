Kubear by Kuberos Innovations: Connected Personal Finance for India

Kuberos Innovations Private Limited (Surat, Gujarat) is an Indian fintech company building Kubear. Kubear connects your income, everyday spending, upcoming bills, EMIs, savings, investments, and life goals into one clear view.

Founder & Director: Sidharth Bothra (MBA, IIM Sirmaur). Kubear is available on Web and Android via Google Play.
