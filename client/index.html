Kubear by Kuberos: A simple money ledger for everyday Indian decisions

Kubear is engineered by Kuberos Innovations Pvt. Ltd. (Surat, Gujarat) to help people bring selected salary, UPI spending, bills, goals, and household commitments into one understandable money picture. It does not move or hold your money.

Kubear is a personal finance assistant, not a bank, broker, investment adviser or payment service. Built with read-only privacy and zero SMS sync.
