Kubear: a read-only financial picture for everyday Indian money decisions

Kubear brings the accounts, bills, spending, investments and household commitments a person chooses to connect into one understandable financial picture. It is designed to help people see what happened, what is coming up and what needs attention without moving their money.

Kubear’s public terms describe it as a personal finance assistant, not a bank, broker, investment adviser or payment service. Its public privacy materials describe read-only access and user control over connected information.
