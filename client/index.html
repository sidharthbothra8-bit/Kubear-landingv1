Kubear: a read-only financial picture for everyday Indian money decisions

Kubear helps people bring selected salary, bills, spending, goals and household commitments into one understandable money picture. It is designed to help people see what happened, what is coming up and what needs attention without moving their money.

Kubear is a personal finance assistant, not a bank, broker, investment adviser or payment service. Its public materials describe read-only access and user control over connected information.
