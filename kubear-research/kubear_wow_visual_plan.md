# Kubear Visual Story Plan: **Money has a week. See the full week.**

## The ambition

The next Kubear marketing site should feel less like a careful finance explainer and more like a **short, satisfying visual film about your money week finally making sense**. It should have the energy and scene-by-scene confidence of Cleo’s public site, but it must remain recognisably Kubear: calmer, more useful, built for Indian salary cycles and household money, and honest about what the product does.

> **Cleo reference, not copy:** Borrow the discipline of a strong point of view, surprising scene changes, large visual moments, product proof and motion that explains a benefit. Do not borrow Cleo’s purple/blue palette, aggressive persona, copy, characters, banking claims or animation artwork.

The core visitor feeling is simple: **“This understands what my money week actually looks like.”** The visual reward is not a flashy investment graph. It is the satisfying moment when salary, UPI, rent, bill, goal and shared-home signals find their right place.

## Behaviour insights to use with restraint

The creative story should meet a real pattern of Indian money behaviour: payments are frequent and increasingly digital, which means small transactions can be easy to make but harder to remember in context. RBI’s 2024 annual-report statistics say UPI recorded 17,220.80 crore transactions during calendar year 2024, while NPCI publishes the platform’s ongoing monthly product statistics.[3] The point for Kubear is not to make a boastful payment statistic; it is to recognise that “UPI everywhere” is a familiar feeling and that people need a way to see a month’s many small moments beside salary, bills and goals.

Research on digital financial literacy also links informed use of digital payments with safer, more deliberate consumer behaviour.[4] The site should therefore reward understanding rather than shame spending. It should say “See what is left” and “Plan your salary before it disappears”, never “You are bad with money” or “we will fix your habits.”

## Audience and product truth

The primary audience is an Indian working adult who gets paid monthly, spends often through UPI, pays cards and bills, has a SIP or a goal, and may coordinate household money. They are not asking for a lecture. They want to answer small, real questions before the month gets messy: “What is due next?”, “How much is actually left?”, “Did we already pay this?”, and “Can I spend this without messing up the plan?”

Kubear’s approved Play Store description supports a useful, distinct role: it helps adults in India record income and spending, plan how salary covers monthly needs, keep bills and goals visible, understand what remains after planned commitments, and manage selected shared home entries while personal entries remain separate.[1] The plan must retain Kubear’s essential boundary: **it does not hold or move money and does not provide regulated financial advice**.[1]

## Selected route: **The Week Comes Together**

This route gives Kubear its own visual movie. It starts with a familiar Monday-to-Sunday money mess, moves through a satisfying gathering moment, and finishes with one clear, human decision. The visual language is bolder than the current Living Ledger concept, but it still does not imply that Kubear moves money, predicts a financial outcome or gives personalised regulated advice.

## Visual system

### Color philosophy

The palette should use **deep ink as the emotional anchor, warm cream as the breathing room, and bright Kubear Orange only for the moments that matter**. The visual page must feel richer and more modern than a beige finance site, but never like a neon trading terminal.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Brand anchor | **Monsoon Ink** | `#092B27` | Main dark sections, app/device framing, high-trust moments, footer. |
| Signature signal | **Kubear Orange** | `#FF6A35` | CTA, connections, selected tab, timeline marker, interactive focus and key numerals. |
| Light base | **Chai Cream** | `#FFF4E6` | Primary background, paper layers, readable long-form content. |
| Soft contrast | **Bill Beige** | `#F0E0C9` | Secondary scene fields, physical paper edges, calm dividers. |
| Positive state | **Mint Receipt** | `#CFE9D8` | “Done”, income received, small confirmed states. Never a primary brand field. |
| Goal state | **Jamun** | `#5D4BD8` | The goals scene, occasional visual surprise and a single app-oriented highlight. |
| Attention state | **Due Red** | `#D24E43` | Due bills and caution labels only. |
| Ink text | **Ledger Black** | `#14312E` | Primary type on light scenes. |

**Color rule:** Orange owns action and connection. Jamun is reserved for one narrative scene, so it creates surprise rather than another permanent brand color. Mint and Due Red act like data, not decoration.

### Typography

The current editorial voice should become more contemporary and more kinetic. Use **Bricolage Grotesque** for display, large statements and oversized numbers. Its variable shapes bring character without pretending to be a mascot. Keep **Manrope** for body and interface text, where it stays clear on small Indian mobile screens. Use **Roboto Mono** in tiny bursts only for dates, “Aaj”, amounts and motion labels; this gives the money system precision without turning the site into a developer dashboard.

Headlines should be short, deliberate and speak the way a person in India talks about money with a friend. Body copy should use one thought per sentence. Product terms such as “Money Spaces” can appear only after a plain-English explanation.

| Copy type | Example | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero | “Salary in. Bills due. UPI everywhere.” | Bricolage ExtraBold, 72–116px desktop, 44–60px mobile, tight line-height. |
| Scene statement | “Home money together. Your money stays yours.” | Bricolage Bold, 48–72px desktop, 36–46px mobile. |
| Plain explanation | “See income, spending, bills and goals in one view.” | Manrope Medium, 18–22px desktop, 16–18px mobile. |
| Money label | “FRI · CARD BILL · ₹2,800” | Roboto Mono/Manrope Bold, 10–12px, uppercased sparingly. |
| CTA | “Open Kubear” | Manrope ExtraBold, 15–16px, never vague “Get started”. |

### Shape, depth and texture

Avoid generic smooth SaaS cards. The building blocks should look like a **modern money desk**: date tabs, folded slips, little payment receipts, goal pins, ruled weekly strips and visible layer edges. Keep corners modest, usually 12–16px, with occasional squared paper tabs. Use soft cream grain, thin ledger rules and saturated ink blocks to create physical depth.

The page must use actual app screenshots from Kubear’s own Play Store or product team as the main product proof. Do not generate fake dashboards. Screens can be cut into sections, layered into paper frames, or animated as panels. Any generated art should be abstract and support the story, such as a dimensional orange connection trail or tactile receipt texture.

## Motion plan

Motion is the product explanation. Each scene has one “wow” movement and a quiet resting state. Do not add constant bouncing, parallax overload or a cartoon assistant. People should still be able to understand the page with motion disabled.

| Motion moment | Experience | Technical approach | Accessibility rule |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hero week assembly** | Salary, UPI, bill, SIP, rent and goal tabs enter from different directions, then resolve around the product screen. | CSS transform/opacity with one SVG motion path; 800–1200ms total, scroll-independent. | Show final complete state instantly in reduced-motion mode. |
| **Copper connector draw** | A copper line traces the relationship between a signal and the money view. | SVG `stroke-dashoffset` or CSS scaleX, one-time play as the scene enters. | No looping. Keep a still visible connector in reduced-motion mode. |
| **Salary ribbon** | A thick orange ribbon starts as salary and splits into monthly needs, bills and a goal marker. | SVG mask/path animation, scroll-triggered but scrub-free. | Use a static labelled allocation illustration when motion is reduced. |
| **Home split** | The visitor drags or taps “Home” and “Personal” tabs into the right view. | Accessible tabs plus Framer Motion layout animation; click remains useful without movement. | Keyboard controls must produce an instant state change. |
| **Goal journey** | The goal marker moves through a monthly path as planned commitments become visible. | Horizontal scroll scene on desktop; vertical story stack on mobile. | No horizontal scroll trap; a visible “next” control works with keyboard. |
| **Buttons** | Copper underline reaches the CTA, then button gives a firm press response. | 140–180ms opacity/transform and 0.97 active scale. | No movement needed to understand the label. |
| **Page transitions** | New routes feel like turning to a fresh ledger sheet. | 160ms fade/translate, route title updates immediately. | Disable entirely under reduced-motion. |

### Motion pacing

The page should have three energy levels. The first 12 seconds are **high energy**: the hero resolves a familiar money mess into a clear view. The middle is **steady and useful**: each new product truth receives one meaningful animation. The close is **quiet confidence**: the final CTA has space, no visual noise and a clean device/application handoff.

| Product explanation sections | **Open Kubear** | `https://kubear.kuberos.in` | **See how it works** | Anchored section or `/how-it-works`. |

| Footer | **Open Kubear** | `https://kubear.kuberos.in` | **Android app** | Google Play listing below. |

**Android destination:** [Google Play: Kubear: Money Manager](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=in.kuberos.kubear&pcampaignid=web_share).

The page should use a real QR code for the Play Store on desktop, with clear labelling. It should never claim an App Store route if none exists. Links to the web app should open in the same browser tab. Google Play should open in a new tab on desktop; on mobile, it should use the standard direct-link behavior.

## Everyday examples and third-party brands

The site should use situations that feel like real Indian money life: a Goa trip fund, a Friday card bill, shared rent, a house-help payment, UPI auto-debits, a wedding gift, a family medical buffer, school fees, a Netflix subscription, food delivery and a month-end “what is left?” check. These examples make the product human without pretending to know a visitor’s finances.

> **Working legal note, not formal legal advice:** A plain-text, factual reference to a third-party product can often be useful for context, but it is not a permission to use a logo, app screenshot, copied product UI, a confusing lookalike, a misleading endorsement, or a claim of partnership. Use the product name only when it is necessary and truthful, keep the use proportionate, add no third-party visual marks, and have counsel review final public brand references in India.

The safest default is generic contextual language, such as “food delivery”, “cab”, “streaming”, “flight booking” or “instant grocery”. A deliberate brand name may appear in a short example only if it makes the point materially clearer, for example: “A few Zomato orders, one Goa flight and your card bill can feel small alone.” It should never be used in a headline, CTA, logo wall, comparison landing page, paid keyword target, product screenshot or visual scene without a separate brand/legal review. The “Goa trip” scene is strong because it is a culturally recognisable goal without relying on another company’s intellectual property.

## Delivery sequence

The work should begin with a **motion prototype rather than a full rewrite**. First, build only the hero and Aaj money-view scene at 390px and 1440px. Confirm that the first five seconds create both recognition and clarity. Second, integrate real Kubear screens into the salary, home and goals scenes. Third, build the trust and app-handoff section. Fourth, apply the system to supporting pages, preserving their page-specific visual stories.

The release is ready only when the actual app links work, app screenshots are current, mobile Android CTA is visible in the first viewport, reduced-motion users see full final states, layout stays readable at 375px through large desktop, no claim exceeds the approved Play Store/product/legal language, and performance is checked on a mid-range Android phone.
