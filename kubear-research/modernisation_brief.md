# Kubear modernisation brief

## Indian-user perspective

The existing preview is clear and thoughtful, but it can feel like a well-designed report before it feels like a product people want to show a friend. A visitor in Bengaluru, Mumbai, Pune, Delhi or a smaller city does not need more finance language. They need a quick feeling of, “Yes, this is exactly my money life.” The strongest immediate cues are salary, UPI, rent, card bill, SIP, home expenses and the question “Can I spend this without messing up the month?”

The revision keeps the trust story, but moves it into short, plain lines. The new content rhythm is **notice it, understand it, do something with it**. All visitor-facing copy uses shorter sentences, direct verbs and familiar money examples. Em dashes are removed from visitor-facing content.

## Modern visual move

The website retains the warm Ledger system but adds a clear “today” layer. The hero moves from a static illustrative financial report into a live-feeling **Aaj ka money picture**: money fragments slide into place, copper paths complete, then a short brief appears. The visual reward is not a flashy chart. It is the satisfying feeling of several familiar money things clicking into one calm view.

The key modern surface is a composed, adaptive ledger rather than an app screenshot. Cards remain paper slips and ledgers, but they have sharper separation, bolder type, subtle depth, brief status pulses and a brighter copper action system. Large warm-yellow fields are reduced so copper stays the point of attention.

## Motion contract

| Moment | Motion | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hero entry | Fragments drift in, copper paths draw, then the money brief lifts into place. | Explain the product in under three seconds. |
| Scroll story | Ledger slips reveal from the nearest margin with a short stagger. | Make the page feel assembled, not dropped in. |
| “Today” brief | Tiny green status dot pulses once; the brief has a subtle highlight sweep. | Add life without implying live customer data. |
| CTA | Copper rule grows toward the button on hover; button presses with a firm 0.97 scale. | Make the next action feel obvious and tactile. |
| Navigation | Compact underline glides to active item on desktop; mobile menu enters with small vertical offset. | Make orientation feel smooth, not decorative. |

All optional movement respects reduced-motion preferences. No animation loops forever except low-key ambient dots, and those are disabled for reduced motion.

## Copy replacements

| Current style | Updated visitor language |
| --- | --- |
| Every money decision needs the whole picture. | **Your money is in many places. See it in one view.** |
| A read-only financial picture | **Your money, clearly together** |
| Read-only by design. Your money does not move through Kubear. | **Just see. No money moves from here.** |
| Every app shows a slice. You carry the connection work. | **Bank, UPI, cards, SIPs. You are doing the maths in your head.** |
| Know where you stand | **See what you really have** |
| Know what is coming | **See what is due next** |
| Know what is already spoken for | **Know what you can spend** |
| Ask the question behind the number | **Ask in simple words** |
| Share the rent. Keep the rest yours. | **Home expenses together. Personal money stays personal.** |
| Stop stitching it together. See the full picture. | **Less checking. More clarity.** |

## Guardrails

The page should never pretend that the illustrative income, balance or reminder is live user data. It should not use “advice”, guarantee savings or imply that Kubear moves money. The first screen needs product proof and a next action, but it should still say “Just see. No money moves from here.” in a clearly visible, plain-language way.
