# Human Money Story: Illustration Production Brief

## Shared character and visual anchors

Create an original fictional Indian young professional named **Aarav**: late twenties, medium-brown skin, short wavy black hair, rust-orange overshirt over an off-white T-shirt, navy trousers and simple white trainers. His roommate **Meera** is also late twenties, medium-brown skin, shoulder-length black hair, deep teal kurta-style overshirt over a light cream top, relaxed dark trousers and simple sandals. They are friendly, contemporary and recognisably Indian without stereotypes. Neither character represents a real person.

Use premium editorial digital gouache with fine ink outlines, hand-cut paper shadows, tactile grain and realistic but simplified proportions. Use deep Juniper, midnight ink, warm ivory, Kubear copper, muted saffron and calm mint. The canvas should be a **text-free illustration only** with enough clean background for webpage copy. Do not show logos, readable app UI, readable financial figures, currency symbols, payment-brand marks, celebrity likenesses or external brand identifiers.

## Scene prompts

### 1. Morning coffee and commute

Create a wide editorial illustration for an Indian personal-finance website. Subject: Aarav pauses at a small independent chai and coffee counter before work, holding a paper cup and a metro card, while a simple phone-payment gesture is visible but the phone screen is abstract. Composition: Aarav positioned on the right half of a warm early-morning street scene, coffee steam drifting upward, a metro entrance suggested in the distance, generous quiet space on the left for web copy. Style: textured digital gouache and editorial ink, polished but human, warm ivory, copper, deep Juniper, muted saffron, no text. Avoid: corporate stock-photo look, payment logos, readable screens, crowds, clutter, stereotypes.

### 2. Salary morning

Create a wide editorial illustration for an Indian personal-finance website. Subject: Aarav sits at a sunlit apartment desk calmly arranging a plain rent envelope, a handwritten-style but unreadable grocery list, a small closed travel tag and a plant. Composition: desk and objects form a gentle path from left to right with Aarav on the right third, open negative space on the left for web copy. Style: tactile editorial digital gouache, warm paper, deep Juniper shadows, copper accents, sophisticated Indian urban-home context, no text. Avoid: readable numbers, finance dashboards, bank logos, generic office stock photography.

### 3. Rent and Goa plan

Create a horizontal editorial illustration for an Indian personal-finance website. Subject: a warm desk vignette where a plain rent envelope and an abstract beach postcard sit on the same pinboard; Aarav reaches in to place a small travel tag beside the envelope. Composition: strong visual route between the monthly envelope on the left and a sunlit Goa-inspired coast-colour postcard on the right, ample clear upper-left area for copy. Style: paper collage plus digital gouache, copper connector line, saffron sunlight, sea-mint accent, deep ink shadows, no text. Avoid: recognisable landmarks, travel-company branding, readable postcard text, tourist-cliché styling.

### 4. Selected home money

Create a wide editorial illustration for an Indian personal-finance website. Subject: Aarav and Meera sit at a home dining table sorting a shared grocery slip and electricity-envelope into a small tray, while Meera’s closed personal notebook remains clearly separate. Composition: two people in a relaxed home scene, shared objects centred, private notebook subtly to one side, broad copy-safe negative space on the outer left. Style: warm editorial gouache, gentle evening lighting, modern Indian home, mint and warm ivory with copper details, no text. Avoid: visible brand packaging, readable financial details, oversimplified family stereotypes.

### 5. You choose the view

Create a wide editorial illustration for an Indian personal-finance website. Subject: Meera calmly chooses three abstract paper cards to place into a translucent glass tray, leaving several other cards outside. Composition: Meera on the right side, cards and tray forming a clear visual boundary, large calm dark-Juniper negative space on the left for light web copy. Style: elegant editorial gouache, midnight and Juniper background, soft mint glass, copper highlights, tactile paper textures, no text. Avoid: security-lock clichés, readable account data, bank cards, brand marks.

### 6. Closing moment

Create a wide editorial illustration for an Indian personal-finance website. Subject: Aarav and Meera leave their tidy evening table, sharing a quiet happy glance; a coffee cup, a small travel tag and a neatly stacked tray remain visible. Composition: characters walking toward the right edge, hero-safe dark Juniper negative space on the left for closing copy, warm saffron evening light at the far right. Style: premium textured digital gouache with editorial ink, modern Indian home, warm and hopeful without being glossy, no text. Avoid: bright fintech-blue gradients, readable screens, third-party brands, celebratory money imagery.

## Foreground-detail prompts

Generate these as separate transparent PNG assets in the same illustration style: rising coffee steam; a copper metro token; a rent-envelope corner; a mint grocery leaf; a saffron travel tag; a small paper receipt with no readable text. Each needs clean alpha, a complete silhouette and no drop shadow. They will animate gently over their matching scene without adding video weight.
